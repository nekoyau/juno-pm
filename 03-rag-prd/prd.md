## Problem & user

Retail PMs need evidence-based prioritization. Juno turns noisy Slack, tickets, and strategy docs into a ranked, cited backlog they can defend.

## Solution overview

**Retrieval strategy:** Long Context

PMs ask questions such as: "What do the SMEs concern the most?" "What action do the SMEs take with the product? - needs summarization from conversations. Going RAG might make the approach over-complicated.

## Retrieval requirements (RAG)

- **Sources:** Sources: Strategy One-Pager (M2 deliverable) + last 40 days of Slack #project_smes + Zoom recordings and transcripts

Quantity: ~1000 Slack threads total (~30 per day). max. 6 Zoom meetings: both Slack and Zoom are the true authority - all other sources are evidence in support.
- **Chunking / indexing:** Keyword
- **Grounding rule:** Every priority Juno produces (P0-P3 or notRecommended) cites at least one strategy clause AND at least three pieces of evidence (Slack permalink, Zoom meeting summary link). The PRD draft renders citations inline as footnotes the human PM can click to verify.
- **Freshness:** Strategy One-Pager: sync on commit (it lives in the team git). Slack + Zoom: refresh weekly on Monday to keep recent customer signal current. There is a cut-off time after a month, maximum 1.5 months - not a real-time channel.

## Requirements

| # | Requirement | Priority | Acceptance criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Retrieval quality and latency | Must | Top-K = 8 retrieval segments per prioritization run. p95 latency target < 3s end-to-end (from "Process" click to ranked PRD draft). At our $0.03/1k token blended cost, this lands at ~$0.07 per Juno run - acceptable for daily PM use. |
| 2 | Fail-safe on empty retrieval | Must | If retrieval returns < 3 relevant segments OR if no strategy doc is loaded, Juno does NOT produce a P0/P1 ranking. Instead it returns a clear banner: "Insufficient evidence to recommend priority - load a strategy document or escalate to PM judgement." This is a feature, not a failure. |
| 3 | Grounded trust | Must | Every priority Juno produces (P0-P3 or notRecommended) cites at least one strategy clause AND at least three pieces of evidence (Slack permalink, Zoom meeting summary link). The PRD draft renders citations inline as footnotes the human PM can click to verify. |

## Out of scope

Decisions that cannot be cited to a source in the knowledge base. Anything Juno is not allowed to retrieve or act on without a human.
